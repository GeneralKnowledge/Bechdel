# The world

**Status:** First version, 15 Nov 2026. Roadmap item P1, the small world bible.

The rule this folder exists for: **the world must make sense when the mystery is removed from it.** If Senna Aldous had never been sentenced, Calloway would still have a housing shortage, two choirs that cannot stand each other, a hospital short of night staff and a Registrar-General two years from retirement. Wexham would still grow pears. Most of the people in the book would still be busy with something else.

This is not an encyclopedia. It holds what a scene might need and cannot find in canon: what things cost, how a week runs, who is quarrelling with whom about what, what people laugh at. Canon ([canon/](../canon/), [mystery/](../mystery/)) still wins wherever the two meet.

## How to use it

Before a scene, look up what the woman would be doing if the investigation did not exist, and let some of it into the scene. Not every scene should touch it, and nothing here needs to be shown.

## What it never does

It never explains or comments on the absence of men. Households, children, work and inheritance are described the way a novel set in our world would describe them: as things everyone already understands. How children come into a household is not described here or anywhere else, because no one in the book would think to describe it.
