# People, with the mystery taken out

**Status:** First version, 15 Nov 2026. What each woman in 1022 would be doing this winter and spring if the letters had never come out of the box. How she thinks is in [voice/MIND_SHEETS.md](../voice/MIND_SHEETS.md); who she is in canon is in [canon/names.md](../canon/names.md). Unnamed walk-ons stay unnamed.

## People uninterested in Senna

The book needs them on the page as much as it needs believers. Among them: Orla until Fen's column; Bryony's packing-house colleagues; Juno's Year Nines; the night staff on Kate's ward; Win Corley; most of Wexham; the bus driver on the 22; Delphine's council officers; the allotment holders at Wallgate; St Hild's choir, who care only that they were not asked.
