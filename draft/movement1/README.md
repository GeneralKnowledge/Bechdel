## Self-audit

**Three tests.** Every scene changes something the storyboard names. Explanation cut in drafting: I-08's hall ("It was not no…"), Kate's pause after "Nobody does", half the shortbread line, all on the cold readers' evidence; I-X lost a sentence that told the reader Jory knew the cloth by its smell, which repeated I-08 too exactly. Kept on purpose: Tamsin's reasoning at the tomb in I-07 ("a hand cannot write twenty-one years after its owner is dead"), because it is her trained habit of saying the next thing, and it ends in her misreading certainty as proof.

**Ending tests.** I-01 ends on the fee and the lie she will tell Juno; I-03 on her hand on the pass; I-04 on a pointless walk to check the young trees; I-05 on Bryony going in before Tamsin reaches the lane; I-06 on Perpetua's hidden wish; I-07 on talking about the altos; I-08 on Kate going back in to tell Ros the committee will be thrilled; I-X on the names about to be read. Four stop when the business is done. I-06's last clause tells what Perpetua hides: watch it at the outside read (watchlist 3).

**Watchlist.** (1) Good lines are spread: Juno, Bryony and Honor get the best, Orla gets dosage-precise sentences, Agnes gets formulae. (2) Reversals: I-05 "drawn is not the same as made" is the movement's one "not this, but that"; I-07 states it again from the other side. Consider cutting I-07's version at revision. (3) Endings: above. (4) Walk-ons: the clerk with green varnish, the eighty-four-year-old reader and Bea talk like themselves. (6) "Kept": used for the ritual (I-08, I-X), the relic rolls, and Juno's "You always mean it" family of habits; not yet a summing-up word for anyone. (7) Nobody reads anything correctly at once: Tamsin misreads the boundary letter and Agnes, Perpetua misreads Delphine, Bryony misreads her own plan, Kate misreads Tamsin's silence. (8) Ros's line about Marit is her own family's, said to Kate, and dropped when the pie comes. (9) Present-day wants and costs: Orla's fees, Bryony's trees, Juno's question, Tamsin's money, Agnes's anniversary, Hesketh's deal. (10) Documents: all under a page.

**Premise.** No sentence explains the Keeping or the absence of men. Honorifics avoided (Gilmour's is a firm). No man-words anywhere in the draft (checked by search).

## Things to check at the outside read

1. **The second layer.** I-X now shares "the porch" with I-03 and I-08, and the brown cloth that "smells of roots" with Marit's madder. If readers name Jory as important after Movement I (questionnaire 13 and 14), soften the cloth first.
2. **I-03's struck word.** In the facsimile, Senna's correction on the Rook line is beside "middle", with the struck word illegible. It is not in the ledger. If the original in the chapel shows the struck word was "eastern edge", it is Senna slipping into the forged line she spent a winter correcting, which is what the 668 letter also does. Proposed as a new clue for Reuben to accept or cut; until then it is texture.
3. **I-06's last clause** may tell what Perpetua's lens should only show.
4. **Order of scenes.** Both pilot readers noticed that the Reading makes sense of Tamsin's catch only if they remembered I-05. The order is right; the outside reader will show whether it lands.
