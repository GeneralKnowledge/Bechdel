# Blind voice pilot

Stage 4. **Packet ready, 15 Nov 2026**: the two scenes (I-05, I-08) are drafted at full length, the four documents are canon, and the shuffled packet and its key are in project files (`bechdel/pilot/packet.md`, `bechdel/pilot/key.md`). Waiting for a reader.

On *Ides*, Books 2 and 0 each wrote three storyboarded scenes at full length before any other prose, shuffled them unlabelled with three scenes from the finished Book 1, and asked an outside reader to rank them and say whether any read as a different writer or knew too much. Both passed, and the first outside reader of Book 0's full draft asked only for a restrained pass. Book 1, which had no pilot, needed a full-book voice pass after an outside reader found every viewpoint thought alike.

This book has no earlier volume to pair against, so the pilot tests the two risks the plan names instead.

## The scenes (proposed)

| Scene | POV or voice | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| I-05 The bundle | Tamsin | The inciting discovery; the main voice |
| I-08 The Reading | Kate | The faith from inside, which must persuade a sceptic |
| Four documents: one Marit diary entry, one Senna letter, the Great Life's death scene, a passage of Dorcas's Account | Document voices | Each must be distinct and worth reading for its own sake |

## The test

One outside reader, ideally a mystery reader with no interest in the premise. Give them the two scenes and the four documents, unlabelled and shuffled, and ask:

1. Rank the six pieces by how much you wanted to keep reading.
2. How many different writers' hands do you think produced the four documents?
3. Pick a sentence from each scene that only that person would think.
4. Did anything explain itself after it had already been shown?
5. Did the faith seem beautiful, foolish, or neither?
6. Did you think about the absence of men? Where?

**Pass:** the two scenes are not both ranked below all four documents (the present must hold its own against the past); the reader hears at least three hands in the documents; the faith reads as beautiful or neither, not foolish; no more than one place where the premise is noticed.
**Fail:** fix the mind sheets, not the scenes, and rerun once with fresh scenes.

The packet and its key go in project files, not in this repository.
