# Open criticisms

Criticisms of the book that are not being acted on now but should be counted when they come up again. The rule comes from Reuben's *Ides of March* project (11 Nov 2026): if we hear the same thing several times, it is time to do something.

**How to count.** Add a source each time a separate read raises the point, with the date and the stamp of the text it read. A point is **due** when three separate reads have raised it and at least one read the text as it now stands. Reads that share a format, a scoring scale or carried-over examples probably come from one reviewer and count as one source until Reuben says otherwise.

**What "do something" means.** A bounded pass on named scenes with a hard stop, judged by the three tests in [voice/MIND_SHEETS.md](../voice/MIND_SHEETS.md) and the questions in [voice/WATCHLIST.md](../voice/WATCHLIST.md). Never a ban or a quota, and never an aim to make the prose less polished.

**Carried over from *Ides* as watch items.** The *Ides* readers found these habits in Reuben's house style. They are not criticisms of this book until a reader of this book raises them, but a single mention here counts as the second source.
