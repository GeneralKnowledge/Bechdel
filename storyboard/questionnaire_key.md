### Reading the open questions

- **13 tests the second layer's plants.** Jory should be *available* but not *salient*. If no reader can recall the porch woman or the alms book when prompted afterwards, the plants are too faint. If most readers name her unprompted in 13, they are too loud: soften C20, C26 or the interlude, one at a time.
- **14.** "She didn't die" is fine (the plan expects clever readers to guess the first layer). "Someone else is in the tomb" after Movement I means the second layer is exposed.
- **16** should find nothing explained twice. A hit is a watchlist question 3 or the explaining narrator.
- **17** should be "no". If yes, which pair: check the mind sheets, not the scenes.
- **18** should be "no", or "only at the start". Any other answer is a place where the premise is being explained.
