# Reader questionnaire

For the outside reader of each stamped movement. Answer from memory after one read, without going back to the text. Short answers are fine; "don't know" is a useful answer. At least one reader per movement should read mysteries and have no interest in the premise. The key is in [questionnaire_key.md](questionnaire_key.md) and is not for the reader.

Write the stamp from the title page here: ____________

## Movement I

1. What are the two sisters arguing about, and what does each of them want?
2. What is Tamsin's job, and where did she work before?
3. What does Calloway believe happened to Senna Aldous, and when?
4. What does Juno want from Tamsin? What does Tamsin say?
5. What is displayed in the Registry Chapel? Describe anything you remember.
6. What does Agnes tell Tamsin?
7. What does Bryony read at the meeting house, and what does she decide to do afterwards?
8. What is in the bundle Bryony gives Tamsin? What did Tamsin notice in it?
9. What is the Cause, and who are Hesketh and Perpetua?
10. Why does Tamsin go back to the chapel after hours, and what does she find?
11. What happens at the Reading at Kate's house? What does Kate ask Tamsin?
12. What is "taking in"?

### Open questions (no right answer)

13. Name up to three people from this part who are not among the main characters.
14. What do you think really happened to Senna? How sure are you?
15. Which scene would you cut if you had to? Which would you keep at any cost?
16. Was there anything you had to read twice, or anything the book explained that you had already understood?
17. Did anyone sound like anyone else?
18. The book is set in a world without men. Did you think about that while reading? Where?

## Movement II

1. What does Tamsin's report to the sisters have to mention, and why does it matter later?
2. What did Tamsin find in the Registry stacks, and what did it tell her about the letter of 668?
3. Who are Kate and Juno to each other? What did they talk about at breakfast?
4. What did Win Corley show Tamsin, and what did Win think of it?
5. How did the letters get into the newspaper? How did Bryony find out?
6. What do Hesketh and Perpetua each think the letters are?
7. What happens in the Hild's Day play? Why does Tamsin leave?
8. What did Tamsin hear at the meeting house, and what did Bryony say to her afterwards?
9. What does Kate ask Tamsin to do, and what does Tamsin say?

### Open questions (no right answer)

10. Who do you think wrote the letters? How sure are you?
11. Which of the women in this part would you trust with a secret?
12. Was there anything you had to read twice, or anything the book explained that you had already understood?
13. Did the present-day story hold you as much as the old documents?

## Movement III

1. What did Tamsin and Perpetua find in the library? What does it say about Senna's death?
2. What did Perpetua notice about the Great Life's death scene?
3. What happened at Honor Bale's rehearsal?
4. What did Tamsin read at Aunt Ros's house? Why did Kate mind?
5. What did Tamsin learn about the ink of the relic rolls?
6. What happened in the survey room? What did Agnes say?
7. What is Tamsin's theory about Senna and Dorcas? Who did she tell?
8. What happened to Tamsin's access to the Registry, and why?
9. What is happening with the orchard sale?

### Open questions (no right answer)

10. Do you believe Tamsin's theory? Why, or why not?
11. Name anyone from this part or earlier who is not one of the main characters.
12. Which scene would you cut if you had to?
13. Did any secondary character explain Tamsin, or the book, to you?

## Movement IV

1. What made Tamsin realise she was wrong?
2. What does "made dead to the Keeping" mean? How did Perpetua know?
3. What were the rolls in the chapel used for in 647?
4. What happened on the frost night, and what did Bryony give Tamsin?
5. What did Dorcas offer Senna in 655, and what did Senna answer?
6. Who was M., and what did her letter say?
7. What did Tamsin and Delphine find in the Teague papers? Who was "B. L."?
8. What did Hesketh decide to do at the anniversary?
9. What did Tamsin find filed under "Consolations of the bereaved"? How did she read it?
10. What did Bryony ask Tamsin after the joint service?

### Open questions (no right answer)

11. Who do you think is in the chapel tomb? How sure are you?
12. Why do you think Marit let Senna go?
13. Is Hesketh right that Senna could still be a saint?
14. Did the investigation ever feel too easy?

## Movement V

1. What happened at Juno's concert, and what did Juno ask Tamsin afterwards?
2. What happened when the tomb was opened? What did the first expert say?
3. Where is Senna buried, and who knew?
4. What did Fen's column say, and what did the second expert say?
5. What did the Aldous family argue about after the column? What did Kate give Tamsin?
6. How did Tamsin work out who is in the tomb? Which documents did she use?
7. What did Hesketh decide, and what will it cost Perpetua?
8. What did Tamsin find in the city archive, and what did Agnes say to her?

### Open questions (no right answer)

9. When did you first suspect the porch woman, if you did? What made you think of her?
10. Was the answer about the tomb fair, looking back?
11. Did you think about the absence of men in this part? Where?

## Movement VI

1. What did Tamsin tell Kate, and how did Kate take it?
2. What happened to the Cause, to Perpetua and to Delphine?
3. How was the orchard settled? What did Orla sell?
4. What did Hesketh offer Tamsin, and why did she refuse?
5. What did Tamsin and Juno do for Jory, and who helped them?
6. What did the Order decide at midsummer?
7. What is the last image of the book?

### Open questions (no right answer)

8. Why do you think Marit let Senna go?
9. Which decision at the end cost the most?
10. Was the ending a rescue?
11. Which woman will you remember?
