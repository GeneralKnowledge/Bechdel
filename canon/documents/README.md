# Documents

The exact wording of in-world documents whose words are clues: a diary line, a tribunal formula, a letter, a hymn verse. They may be drafted before the prose (see [docs/WORKING_GUIDE.md](../../docs/WORKING_GUIDE.md)) and are canon once written. One file per document, named by date and source, for example `0646_marit_diary_winter.md`. No document longer than a page.

Fixed already, from the plan:

- Tribunal register, 647: "Senna Aldous, registrar, made dead to the Keeping, for the rolls."
- Steward's accounts, 647: "B. L., for her word."
- Chapel alms book, 647: "Jory of the porch, bread and a blanket."
- Marit's diary, 646: "S. tired and secret all winter."
- Marit's letter, 661: "The children are well. Wren has your hand. Do not write again."
- Marit's petition, 655: "in case she is not dead to me".
- Dorcas's Account, 660: "was made dead for the rolls, and the house of the Keeping was the poorer".
- Gravestone, Wexham, 678: "Senna, schoolmistress, kept by those who knew her".
- School record, Wexham, 647: "Senna, from Calloway, to teach letters".
- Infirmary book, 631: "a dyeworks hand, leg broken in a fall from the vats, discharged unhealed".
- Benedicta's note: "the eastern entries, as we discussed".
- Translation record, about 800: "the grave signed with her hand".
- Dorcas's will, 694: "to the schoolhouse of Wexham, for pens".
- The hymn: *Write her where the fire is kept, / Read her when the house is still; / Though the hand that wrote her fail, / Another hand will, another will.*
