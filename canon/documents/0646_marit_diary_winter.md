# Marit's diary, winter 646 to 647

**Source:** Aldous family papers, held by Rosamund Aldous (Aunt Ros). Marit dates her entries by day and month and writes the year once, at the top of the page. Read aloud at the Reading in I-08 (Kate reads the entries of 2 December and 28 February); read by Tamsin in III-05.

**Clues:** C06 ("S. tired and secret all winter"), C26 (the woman on the chapel porch). Settled wording, 15 Nov 2026. Per Reuben (15 Nov), the porch entry does not mention a leg, the vats or a name.

---

*646*

**3 November.** Madder for the Pryor girls, twelve lengths. W. lost a tooth and wants it written in the roll. Told her teeth are not kept. She says Tobi's will be.

**18 November.** Rain. Nothing dries. Mother says the yard is a pond with a business attached.

**2 December.** Spoiled madder to the woman on the chapel porch, three lengths, the brown end of the batch. She knew the colour by the smell before I had it unrolled. Said it would do for a blanket if nobody looked. Nobody does.

**11 December.** Tobi coughing. Honey and the kettle. S. in at two again with ink to the wrist. The Registry is behind, she says. The Registry has been behind since Keeper Carrow was a girl.

**20 December.** Lard for the pie. W. to read the first name at the Reading if she can say it without laughing.

*647*

**2 January.** W. said it. Laughed after.

**28 February.** Woad up a penny. The Danby order sent back, wrong blue, which it was not. S. tired and secret all winter. I have stopped asking. W. says she talks in her sleep, and it is numbers.
