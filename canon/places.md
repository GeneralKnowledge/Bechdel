## The orchard boundary

Fixed so the clues hold (Movement II storyboard, 15 Nov):

- **The forged entry (645)**, in the roll Carrow altered, puts the line between the two parcels along the **eastern edge of the pear row**. That roll was cancelled in 649 and has been in a locked press in the Registry stacks ever since, as a struck volume (II-02).
- **The corrected entry**, in Senna's copy, the roll on display in the chapel since 649 and the legal roll ever since, runs the line **down the middle of the pear row**, as the older surveys of 590 and 612 do.
- **The letter of 668** describes the forged line, and so does the Rook family's own belief that "the Rooks have had the pear row since the Return". Bryony's evidence supports the line the Teagues forged.
- **The settlement (VI-03)** confirms the corrected line. The orchard is not sold, so it does not matter to anyone but Bryony that the row is split.

## Distances (proposed)

- Calloway to Wexham: about forty minutes by bus or car now; most of a day on foot in 647. Close enough for Senna to stay away by choice, which is the point.
